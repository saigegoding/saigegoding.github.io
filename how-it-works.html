Questions

What does it cost?

Every plan is quoted personally after the first chat, because dinner for eight and a night for forty are very different things. You'll see the full price before anything is booked.

How far ahead should I book?

The earlier the better: a few weeks ahead gives the most choice of rooms and tables. Last-minute? Ask anyway.

Can you plan a surprise?

Yes. I spent five years handling a private family's plans with complete discretion. I'm very good at keeping secrets.

Can I bring my own ideas?

Please do. A Pinterest board, a screenshot, the restaurant you've always wanted to try: it all helps.

What if plans change?

Tell me as soon as you know. Dates, guest numbers and stops can usually be adjusted. Some venues have their own cancellation rules, and I'll always tell you about them up front.

Is it only New York?

For now, yes. New York is where my network is, and that's what makes the difference.
